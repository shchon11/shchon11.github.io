Before the RGB.

RIFT · DriveRAW · ISP-stage research

I develop learned RAW-to-detector frontends and lead controlled experiments across datasets, ISP stages and pretraining regimes. The work includes paired RAW/RGB data acquisition, annotation tools, MMDetection baseline integration, ablations and cross-domain evaluation.

Research manuscripts and experimental systems. Related public tooling includes RAW-assisted auto-labeling.

Research contributions
  • RIFT / DriveRAW: task-specific RAW representations, paired low-light driving data, and common evaluation of learned ISP baselines.
  • ISP-stage study: controlled RAW-to-sRGB and pretraining experiments, alignment checks, exposure and bit-depth analysis.
  • Research tools: assisted annotation, cross-ISP pseudo-label fusion, and a RAW-teacher/RGB-student uncertainty-distillation prototype.
