Good autonomy starts
with understanding the signal.

I'm an Automotive Engineering undergraduate at Hanyang University and a research intern at the Intelligent Robotics and Computer Vision Laboratory, advised by Prof. Soonmin Hwang. My work spans RAW image perception, sensor calibration, and learning-based autonomous driving.

I like working across the whole loop: collect the data, understand the failure, build the system, and test what actually changed.
